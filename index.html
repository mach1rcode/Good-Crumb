    The hydration counts the flour and water in your starter. The starter is fed with equal weights of flour and water.

    Cups are less exact than a scale. Spoon the flour into the cup and level it. Do not pack it. If the dough is too stiff or too wet, add water or flour one tablespoon at a time. The salt is fine sea salt or table salt. For Diamond Crystal kosher salt, use 2 times the volume.
