# Good Crumb

A sourdough app that does one thing: one loaf, done right. It gives you the recipe, the timers, and feedback on the result.

Open: https://mach1rcode.github.io/Good-Crumb/

- Hydration calculator in ounces, grams, or cups. It counts the flour and water in the starter.
- A timer for each stage. The times change with your dough and kitchen temperatures.
- Photo feedback on the crust or the cut crumb. The app measures the photo on your phone. No account is necessary.
- A bake log. Your answer after each loaf changes the bulk time for the next bake.

## Add to an iPhone Home Screen

1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

The app is one file, `index.html`. It needs no build step.
